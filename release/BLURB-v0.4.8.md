# v0.4.8 — release blurb (draft)

_Draft. Written as the window fills, not at tag time -- the moment a change lands is the
only moment anyone remembers why it mattered._

## What is in it so far

Armor sets stop eating a tight seed alive. One randomized set item now delivers every generated
piece in that family—including altered variants—while reconnect-safe pacing fills in only members
the character does not already own. Exact duplicate weapons, both trick mirrors, and Sacrificial
Twigs also stop consuming scarce pool slots.

## What carried over from v0.4.6

This window opened 39 commits past v0.4.6 because v0.4.7 was never tagged. Those accumulated changes
remain owed to the eventual v0.4.8 release notes; stable correctly stays on the last real tag rather
than treating an open window as a shipped release.

## For whoever writes the real one

The v0.4.3 blurb is the model: lead with what changed at the table, not with the option
name. Its opening line -- "You can get BK'ed now, and that is the point" -- says what a
player will feel before it says what was built, and that is the right order.
